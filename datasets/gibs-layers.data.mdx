  #### MODIS_Aqua_Aerosol_Optical_Depth_3km
  Aerosol Optical Depth (3km, MODIS, Aqua) provides measurements of aerosol optical thickness at 3km spatial resolution from the Aqua Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS) instrument. Aerosol optical depth is a measure of the extinction of solar radiation by atmospheric aerosols, indicating the amount of aerosols distributed within a column of air from the Earth's surface to the top of the atmosphere.

  #### OMI_SO2_Upper_Troposphere_and_Stratosphere
  Sulfur Dioxide in the Upper Troposphere and Stratosphere from the Ozone Monitoring Instrument (OMI) aboard NASA's Aura satellite. This product provides daily measurements of SO2 column amounts at higher altitudes, primarily detecting volcanic emissions and major pollution plumes that reach the upper troposphere and stratosphere. OMI provides global coverage with high sensitivity to SO2 at these altitudes.

  Aerosol Optical Depth from the Ozone Monitoring Instrument (OMI) aboard NASA's Aura satellite. This product provides daily measurements of aerosol optical thickness, which is a measure of the extinction of solar radiation by atmospheric aerosols.   #### MERRA2_Aerosol_Optical_Depth_Analysis_Monthly
Monthly Aerosol Optical Depth Analysis from the Modern-Era Retrospective analysis for Research and Applications, Version 2 (MERRA-2). MERRA-2 is a NASA atmospheric reanalysis that begins in 1980 and continues to present. It provides a comprehensive record of Earth's weather and climate by combining observations with numerical models to produce a consistent, gridded dataset of atmospheric variables including aerosol properties.

  Monthly averaged Aerosol Optical Depth at green wavelength (558 nm) from the Multi-angle Imaging SpectroRadiometer (MISR) aboard NASA's Terra satellite.
