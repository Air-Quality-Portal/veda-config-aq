  #### MODIS_Aqua_Aerosol_Optical_Depth_3km
  Aerosol Optical Depth (3km, MODIS, Aqua) provides measurements of aerosol optical thickness at 3km spatial resolution from the Aqua Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS) instrument. Aerosol optical depth is a measure of the extinction of solar radiation by atmospheric aerosols, indicating the amount of aerosols distributed within a column of air from the Earth's surface to the top of the atmosphere.    
  </Prose>

  #### MODIS_Combined_Value_Added_AOD
  ##### Data has gaps (non-periodic)
  Aerosol Optical Depth (Value Added, MODIS, Combined Aqua and Terra) provides combined measurements of aerosol optical thickness from both the Terra and Aqua MODIS instruments. This value-added product merges data from both satellites to provide enhanced temporal coverage and improved data quality. Aerosol optical depth is a measure of the extinction of solar radiation by atmospheric aerosols, indicating the amount of aerosols distributed within a column of air from the Earth's surface to the top of the atmosphere.  
  </Prose>

  Aerosol Optical Thickness at 550nm wavelength from the SeaWiFS Data Bank (SWDB).
